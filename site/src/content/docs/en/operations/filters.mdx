title: Filters and "Where clients go"
description: Traffic filters on the nodes, per-subscription stats by site (off by default) and blocking a site for everyone with one tap.
---

Traffic filters are a built-in tool: **Addons → Built in → Traffic filters**. A node applies them itself, within seconds of saving. A node older than the panel skips them: update it on the **Nodes** page.

## Outgoing traffic

The **Outgoing traffic** card: the nodes refuse users' traffic to what is listed here. The node's own private networks and port 25 are always closed, even without this filter, and on the server's own address only [some ports](/mikan/en/nodes/hardening/#open-ports-of-the-server) are open.

- **Close the mail ports**: a VPN address that sends mail is on blacklists within hours.
- **Ports**: one per line, ranges too, for example `6881-6889`.
- **Networks and addresses**: one per line, for example `203.0.113.0/24` or `2001:db8::/32`.
- **Domains**: one per line, with their subdomains: `example.com` also closes `mail.example.com`.

## Incoming connections

The **Incoming connections** card: the nodes check where connections come from. The list of networks works in two modes: "Deny these networks" or "Allow only these networks". In the second one every other user loses access, so be careful. The panel's other nodes (cascade) are always let in. With a CDN or a TCP proxy in front of a node, the node sees its address, not the user's: list its networks or leave the check off. For a protocol [behind a CDN](/mikan/en/protocols/cdn/) the node takes the real client address from a header.

## Where clients go

:::note[New in 0.5.2.0]
Stats by site are new in 0.5.2.0.
:::

The nodes can count each subscription's traffic by site: by the registrable domain or by the address. It helps to see what exactly a client who takes the whole channel downloads or watches, and to block what you do not want.

### What is kept and how to turn it on

The **Where clients go** card on the filters page, **off** by default.

- **Count traffic by site**: turns the counting on the nodes on. The node works a little harder, because connections go through a counter.
- **Keep, days**: from 1 to 90. Older data is removed once a day.

Only totals are kept: how many bytes per site for each subscription per day. **No times, client addresses or single connections.** A node older than the panel does not count sites.

### Where it shows

The client's card has a **Where it goes** block: sites by falling traffic with a bar. The period: 1, 7 or 30 days. Small sites are gathered into an "other sites" row. While counting is off, the block is hidden if there is no data.

### Block a site for everyone

Each domain in the list has a "Block the site for everyone" button. It adds the domain to the **Domains** of the outgoing traffic filter and turns that filter on if it was off: the nodes let nobody there, subdomains included. An already blocked domain has a tick in place of the button. Addresses have no button: add them to "Networks and addresses" by hand.

### API

`GET /api/v1/traffic-detail` and `PUT /api/v1/traffic-detail` (turn on or off, days kept), `GET /api/v1/users/{id}/sites?days=7` (a subscription's sites).
