## Where mikan differs

### The core

The other panels are all built around Xray. mikan's nodes run on [Citrus](/mikan/en/start/citrus/), our mihomo fork. The node embeds it as a library instead of running a separate process, so:

- traffic is counted per connection of every user, inside the core;
- speed, connection and quota limits hold on the node itself;
- users are added and removed without restarting the node.

This applies to protocols where every user has a key of their own. Sudoku, Snell and Shadowsocks-2022 with ChaCha20 use one key for everyone and have no accounting or limits; see [the protocol list](/mikan/en/protocols/presets/#list).

The protocols differ too. Besides VLESS REALITY, Trojan and Shadowsocks there are Hysteria2, TUIC, AnyTLS, TrustTunnel, Mieru and others from the [presets](/mikan/en/protocols/presets/). Citrus's protocols are tested against Xray and mihomo clients. Which app gets which protocol depends on the protocol: VLESS, Trojan, Hysteria2 and others reach both Xray-based apps (Happ, v2RayTun) and Clash apps, while TrustTunnel, Mieru and a few more reach Clash apps only. See [Who gets what](/mikan/en/protocols/presets/#who-gets-what).

### Sales out of the box

None of the four panels has customer payments in its code. Selling is left to separate bots, such as [Bedolaga](/mikan/en/operations/import/#the-bedolaga-sales-bot) for Remnawave. In mikan it is part of the panel:

- a Telegram bot and a Mini App;
- plans, a trial, promo codes, traffic packages and a balance;
- payment with Telegram Stars and payment addons.

See [Plans](/mikan/en/billing/plans/) and [Telegram bot](/mikan/en/billing/telegram/).

### The subscription

As in Remnawave, each app gets a format it can run: Clash, sing-box, Xray JSON or links. On top of that, mikan has:

- [response rules](/mikan/en/subscriptions/clients/#response-rules) by request headers;
- templates per core;
- a subscription [request log](/mikan/en/subscriptions/clients/#request-log);
- the [what the client gets](/mikan/en/subscriptions/clients/#what-the-client-gets) screen.

### What mikan does not have yet

- **Several admins with different rights.** Today there is one admin, plus Read and Full access API keys and temporary [support access](/mikan/en/operations/support-access/). Roles are on the [roadmap](https://github.com/users/Miroshka000/projects/2) (#283). PasarGuard has them already.
- **WireGuard and AmneziaWG on the nodes.** PasarGuard and 3x-ui have them; for mikan they are on the roadmap (#297).
- **More UI languages.** mikan has two, while 3x-ui has 14.

## Moving over

mikan imports users from Marzban, PasarGuard, Remnawave and 3x-ui, and accounts, balances and history from the Bedolaga bot. Old subscription links keep working. See [Migration](/mikan/en/start/migrate/) and [Import](/mikan/en/operations/import/).
