mikan is free and open source. Features the community asks for are taken into work once their sum is collected. Below are the open goals: pick the one you need and support it on Tribute.

How it works:

1. Each goal has a sum. While it is collecting, the card shows how much is raised.
2. Once the sum is collected, the goal goes into work.
3. The feature ships in a release, and the goal moves to the done list with its version.

A donation is voluntary support of an open source project, not a purchase. We promise no dates, but the money collected for a goal goes into building it.

Goals and sums live in [`.github/goals.json`](https://github.com/o0hhs/mikanaddons/blob/main/.github/goals.json) in the repository. The panel shows the same goals: **Settings → System**.
