Subscribers buy and renew access in the bot and Mini App. The panel creates or extends the subscription as soon as the payment system confirms the payment, and the bot sends the link.

## Turn on selling

<Steps>

1. **Settings → General → Selling subscriptions**, or the "Start selling" button in the **Payments** section. On new panels selling is off.

2. In **Plans**, mark plans "On sale" and set a price in Stars and/or rubles.

3. In **Payments → Accepting payments**, turn on Telegram Stars (it only needs a working bot) or add a payment method from the marketplace.

</Steps>

The same place has the "Sell to new people" and "Renewal resets traffic" switches. A renewal adds the plan's term after the current one and, unless switched off, starts a new traffic period. The leftover of the last period goes into the new one if that is turned on in the [subscription rules](/mikan/en/billing/lifecycle/#leftover-traffic).

:::note[New in 0.5.2.1]
Carrying the leftover over, and taking it back with a refund (see [History and refunds](#history-and-refunds)), are new in 0.5.2.1.
:::

If no plan with a price for an enabled payment method is on sale, the **Payments** section warns you: the bot will only tell people to message support.

With selling off, the bot and Mini App sell nothing, the **Payments** section disappears from the menu, and plan prices are hidden. Invoices issued earlier are still applied.

## Payment method marketplace

**Payments → Payment methods from the marketplace → Add** shows the signed [getmikan/marketplace](https://github.com/getmikan/marketplace) catalog: to begin with, YooKassa (cards and SBP) and CryptoBot (cryptocurrency). The "Install" button puts the adapter on the server as a separate container. Its card has a settings form, the notification URL you need to enter in the payment system's dashboard, and a switch.

The panel never trusts a single notification from a payment system: it asks the adapter for the invoice and checks the amount and currency against its own payment.

On the server the same is done with commands:

```bash
mikan addon list               # what is running and what is in the catalog
mikan addon install yookassa   # install or update an adapter
mikan addon remove yookassa    # stop and remove; settings and history stay
```

Adapters are installed only by the mikan program on the host: the panel itself does not see Docker.

## Balance and campaigns

0.5.2.0 adds two more things to **Payments**:

- **Clients' balance**: a client tops up an internal balance by a transfer and a screenshot, you approve the request on the **Top-ups** tab, and the balance pays for subscriptions, renewals, packages and devices. Its settings are on the **Payment methods** tab, details in [Clients' balance](/mikan/en/billing/balance/).
- **Campaigns**: links to the bot with a code for adverts and the stats by them, see [Ad campaigns](/mikan/en/billing/campaigns/).

## History and refunds

**Payments** has a payment history. With the "Refund" button you can refund an applied Telegram Stars payment or a payment from the [clients' balance](/mikan/en/billing/balance/) (in 0.5.2.0; the money goes back to the balance). A refund takes back what the payment gave:

- a new subscription is disabled, but not deleted;
- a renewal loses the term it bought, and if nothing changed after the payment, the previous plan and limits are restored;
- a traffic package is deleted;
- the leftover of the last period that this payment carried over is taken away with it;
- a promo code can be used again.

Traffic reset by a renewal is not restored. Refunds that Telegram makes on its own are handled the same way. The buyer gets a message in the bot.
