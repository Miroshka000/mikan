## Plans

**Plans** section, "Plans" tab. A plan has:

- **Traffic per period** in GB, or unlimited.
- **Term**: "In days" (0 means no expiry) or "To a day of the month": N months and a billing day. The first term ends on the nearest such day, but not sooner than 15 days from now. In a month without that day, the term ends on the last day.
- **Term from the first connection**: a switch under the term, which appears when the term has an end. The term of new subscriptions on the plan starts when the client first connects, not when the subscription is made, see [Subscription lifecycle](/mikan/en/billing/lifecycle/#term-from-the-first-connection).
- **Devices at once**: from 1 to 100, or unlimited.
- **Speed** in Mbit/s, or unlimited: each way, for all of the user's devices together. A user's own speed is set in their card next to the plan: 10, 25, 50, 100, other or ∞. Each node holds the cap on its own, and with [channel sharing](/mikan/en/nodes/nodes/#share-the-channel-evenly) the plan's speed stays the ceiling.
- **When to reset traffic**: every 30 days, on the 1st (or on the billing day), never.
- **Leftover traffic**: whether what is left unused goes into the new period on a renewal and on a move to this plan: "As in the rules", "Carry over" or "Do not carry over". The field is there for plans with a traffic limit, details in [Subscription lifecycle](/mikan/en/billing/lifecycle/#leftover-traffic).
- **Price label**: shown only in the panel.
- **Description for buyers**: up to 300 characters and 5 lines of plain text. When the limits do not show what sets plans apart ("with white lists", "without"), say it here: the description appears in the bot and the Mini App next to the line built from the limits. Markup does not work, line breaks are kept. An empty description changes nothing.
- **What is included**: [server groups](/mikan/en/billing/pools/) (on the main traffic, with a limit of their own, unlimited or not included) and packages as a gift with a payment. The preview shows what the buyer sees.

The **Selling** block: the "On sale" switch, a price in Telegram Stars and in rubles (at least one is required). **More terms** adds up to 12 options, for example 7, 30 and 90 days, each with its own price. Limits are not multiplied, and traffic resets by the plan's rule. The buyer picks a term in the bot and the Mini App, and the invoice remembers the term and price it was issued with.

An archived plan cannot be chosen for new users; nothing changes for existing ones.

## Users

"New user": a name, an optional contact and a plan. The term, traffic and device limit come from the plan and can be changed later in the card. The "Create and copy the link" button copies the subscription link right away. If the plan's term has an end, a "Term from the first connection" switch sits under the plan choice: it repeats the plan's setting and can be changed for this client.

In the user card:

- **Rename**: the pencil next to the name or **More actions → Rename**. A name is one line of up to 100 characters. The subscription link, keys and devices stay the same; the journal keeps the old name.
- **Extend**: by 7, 30, 90 days or a year, or, with a billing day, by 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. There is also an exact end date and "No expiry".
- **Billing day**: extending by a month runs the term to this day.
- **Change plan**: limits come from the new plan, the term starts again from today, and used traffic is kept.
- **Next plan**: in the "Plan" block, set the plan the client moves to by itself when the term ends, see [Subscription lifecycle](/mikan/en/billing/lifecycle/#next-plan).
- **Reset traffic**, **Disable**.
- **More actions → Reissue the link**: the old link and all devices stop working immediately, along with any old links left after an import.
- **Add traffic**: a package as a gift or as compensation.
- **Telegram → Unlink**, a torrent ban and "Do not check for torrents".

The list can be filtered by state: active, expiring, limit reached, expired, disabled. Search works on name, contact, tag and note. With rows selected, you can do "+1 month", reset traffic, disable and delete: before doing it the panel shows what will change and waits for a confirmation, see [Preview of bulk actions](/mikan/en/billing/lifecycle/#preview-of-bulk-actions). The **Rules** button in the page header opens the general subscription rules: carrying leftover traffic over and removing inactive subscriptions, see [Subscription lifecycle](/mikan/en/billing/lifecycle/).

Since version 0.5.0.3 there are also folders, a user source (created in the panel, bought in the bot, free trial, import) and hidden users. Hiding only removes the row from the list: the subscription, payment and nodes work as usual.

## Devices

Without binding, a device is an IP address with an active connection, and its place frees up about a minute after it disconnects.

**Settings → Subscription → Devices → Bind subscriptions to devices** protects against key resale:

- each device gets its own keys, and the device limit becomes the number of places;
- an extra device sees that all places are taken instead of servers;
- apps without a device ID share one place.

With the **Only apps with a device ID** switch, other apps get no subscription. Happ, Koala Clash, INCY, v2RayTun, FlClashX, ClashFest and SlothClash (unless it is turned off in its settings) send a device ID.

A subscriber unbinds devices themselves on the subscription page, in the Mini App or in the bot, under the rules of **Settings → Subscription → Devices**: how many devices per period (one a day by default; a week, a month, your own number, no limit, or "Not allowed": then only an administrator unbinds) and how soon an unbound device may connect again (a day by default, so that an app left open on it does not take the place back; "Never": until an administrator lets it). An administrator unbinds any time in the user card, and sees the pause after an unbind among the banned devices, where it can be lifted earlier. An unbound device is cut off immediately. Only bound devices take places, so a phone moving from Wi-Fi to mobile data does not turn into two devices.

### How the device limit counts

On a plan and on each client the device limit counts in one of two ways (in the plan form and in the client card, the Devices block):

- **Bound devices**: as described above, each device with an ID takes a place, and no more than the limit can be bound.
- **Online at once**: any number of devices may be bound, and only the ones connected at the same moment count. The server turns the extra ones away; it tells devices apart by address, so a phone on mobile data and a laptop at home count as two. Handy when a client cares more about switching between their own devices than about staying within places. The bot, the Mini App and the subscription page say "up to N at once".

A client takes the plan's mode with its other limits when created, paid for or moved to another plan, and the client card can change it separately.

### A new link by the subscriber

In the bot (Subscription → "🔄 New link") and in the Mini App ("Change the link") a subscriber replaces the subscription link themselves if it fell into the wrong hands. The old link and the keys of all their devices stop working at once, so a confirmation comes first, and the new link after it: it has to be added to the apps. The administrator sets the limit in **Settings → Subscription → Devices → New link by the client**: "Not allowed" (only the administrator), or so many a day, a week, a month or an own number of days (once a day by default), or no limit. At the limit the button is gone and a line tells when it comes back. The audit log gets a `user.reissue` entry by the subscriber. The administrator reissues the link in the client card any time.

### Device names

A device is called what its app reports: the model, the system or the app itself. The administrator (the pencil in the user card) or the subscriber (on the subscription page and in the Mini App) can give it a name of up to 40 characters. An empty name brings back the one from the app. The bot lists devices by these names.

### Banning a device

An unbound device takes a place again on its next subscription update. To stop that, press **Ban** next to the device in the card:

- the device is cut off at once and its keys burn;
- it can no longer bind to this user by its ID, not with the same link and not after a reissue;
- instead of servers the app shows "⛔ This device is blocked", with binding turned off too.

A ban covers one user: on someone else's subscription the device works. Only a device with an ID can be banned. The "Connection addresses" rows and the shared place of apps without an ID cannot: one address is often shared by many people. The **Banned** list in the card shows when and by whom, and **Unban** lifts the ban. A subscriber cannot lift it. Bans and unbans go to the journal.

## Extra devices

:::note[New in 0.5.2.0]
Buying extra devices is new in 0.5.2.0.
:::

A client can buy one more device for a subscription in the bot and the Mini App, without changing the plan or renewing it.

### Set it up

The plan form has an **Extra devices** block. The plan must have a device limit: with no limit there is nothing to buy.

- **How many may be bought**: from 0 to 50. 0 means no buying.
- **Device price, Stars** and **Device price, ₽**: at least one when buying is on. The price is for one device. The ruble price can be paid from the [balance](/mikan/en/billing/balance/) too.

In **Payments → Sales rules** the switch **Bought devices stay with renewals** chooses how long a bought device lasts:

- **On**: the device stays after a renewal, and a renewal costs more by the price of the bought devices.
- **Off**: the device lasts to the end of the subscription's paid term and goes with it.

### How a client buys

The subscription card in the bot has a **📱 Buy one more device** button. The bot shows how many devices there are now and how many there will be, until when the device lasts (or that it stays with renewals), how many more can be bought, and the payment methods. After the payment the subscription's device limit grows by one at once. Promo codes do not apply to buying a device.

The button is missing if the subscription has no plan, is off or over, the plan has no price for the payment methods that are on, or the client has bought all that is allowed.

A device payment shows in the [payment history](/mikan/en/billing/payments/#history-and-refunds) as "Device". It can be refunded like other payments: the device is taken off and the buyer gets a message.

## Another subscription in the bot

:::note[New in 0.5.2.0]
Buying a second subscription is new in 0.5.2.0.
:::

One Telegram account can hold up to five subscriptions. In the bot the **🔁 Subscriptions** button opens their list, and the list has **➕ Another subscription**: the bot offers the plans as for a first purchase and, once paid, adds the new subscription to the account.

The button shows while there are fewer than five subscriptions, "Sell to new people" is on ([Payments → Sales rules](/mikan/en/billing/payments/)) and at least one plan is for sale. With one subscription the "Subscriptions" button appears as soon as a second one can be bought.
