# The mikan name and logo

The code of mikan is free under the GNU GPL v3. The name **mikan**, the mikan logo and the mandarin mascot are not: the license does not grant them (GPL v3 section 7(e), see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).

## You may

- Write about mikan, review it, teach it and link to it by its name.
- Say that your own product works with mikan, or is based on mikan, in plain words ("a fork of mikan", "based on mikan").
- Show the logo next to such a text, unchanged, when it points to this project.

## You may not

- Call your fork, build, service or bot "mikan", or a name that looks like it, or use the logo or the mascot for it.
- Present your build as an official mikan release, or as made, checked or supported by the mikan project.
- Use the name or the logo in a way that makes people think they are dealing with the mikan project when they are not.

## Official sources

The official mikan is only the repository [Miroshka000/mikan](https://github.com/Miroshka000/mikan), the image `ghcr.io/miroshka000/mikan` and the releases signed with the mikan key. Official addons come only from [getmikan/marketplace](https://github.com/getmikan/marketplace).

Questions about the name and the logo: the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/mikanvpn).
